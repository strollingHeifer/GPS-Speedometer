GNSS Doppler & Signal Confidence

1. How Hardware GNSS Doppler Velocity Works

When your smartphone has a clear sky view, its GNSS receiver measures the carrier-phase Doppler frequency shift of satellite radio signals (coords.speed, badged as DOPPLER). Doppler velocity is typically accurate to within ±0.18 km/h (±0.05 m/s) and responds immediately without differentiation lag.

2. Multi-Factor Signal Confidence (0–100%)

When coords.speed is unavailable (such as indoors or in tunnels), the engine falls back to accuracy-gated Haversine geodesic distance (Δd/Δt, badged as CALC) with exponential smoothing (α = 0.35) and a 1.5G multipath spike filter. Confidence combines:

  • Excellent (≤5.5 m, ≥0.75 Hz): Active multi-constellation GNSS Doppler lock.
  • Good (5.5–12 m, ≥0.35 Hz): Reliable GNSS fix.
  • Fair (12–30 m): Urban canyon multipath or partial sky view.
  • Poor (>30 m or Fix Age >5s): Cell/Wi-Fi fallback or tunnel loss.
